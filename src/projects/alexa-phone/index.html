what the fuck is an alexa phone

very good question, dear reader! what IS the alexa phone? while at emfcamp 2026 i came across this beautiful monstrosity in the swap shop. i was looking for a DECT phone to use with the local telephone network, as the two i had brought were not compatible... not only did this phone support DECT, it had an incredibly suspicious button right on the face. i HAD to have it.

the BT Advanced Digital Home Phone with Alexa is a traditional home phone handset (ala analogue landline) that your grandma has because she refuses to get a smartphone, with the added discomfort of having a voice assistant permanently baked in. it uses DECT to communicate wirelessly, like most cordless landlines of the last three decades.

a little about DECT

Digital Enhanced Cordless Telecommunications (DECT) is a wireless communications standard originally intended as a way for computers to roam wirelessly between networked basestations.

think of it as WiFi's predecessor! it has many applications, with cordless analogue phones being the most popular by far. no more silly curly wires preventing you wandering around while on the phone!

DECT has had many extensions and upgrades over the years, now supporting wideband audio codecs, caller ID, phonebooks, call transfers, and more. while DECT is a standard, lots of manufacturers implement their own features and proprietary extensions. not all phones support every feature from every basestation

DECT operates in the 1880-1900MHz band @ 10mW in europe, and 1920-1930 MHz (DECT 6.0) @ 4mW in north america
